Remote Sensing

Seeing Earth Through Data

Remote sensing transforms observations of the Earth's surface into measurable data. This section explores satellite imagery, electromagnetic radiation, Earth observation, digital image processing, geospatial analysis, and machine learning.

Writing inspiration: The idea that a satellite image is not merely a photograph, but a collection of measurements containing physical and scientific information.

Topics will range from the physics behind remote sensing to computational approaches for extracting information from large-scale Earth observation data.

Speech Processing

From Sound to Intelligence

Speech is a complex signal containing information about language, speakers, emotion, and communication. This section explores the mathematical and computational methods used to analyse speech and audio.

Writing inspiration: The fascinating transformation of a physical sound wave into a numerical signal and eventually into information that a machine can understand.

Topics may include sampling, Fourier analysis, spectrograms, MFCCs, feature extraction, speech recognition, speaker analysis, and machine learning.

Image Processing

Understanding Images as Data

Digital images are numerical structures rather than simply visual objects. This section explores how mathematical and computational techniques can be used to enhance, transform, analyse, and interpret images.

Writing inspiration: The realization that every image can be interpreted as structured numerical information and analysed through mathematics.

Topics include image representation, filtering, convolution, Fourier transforms, segmentation, feature extraction, computer vision, and machine learning.

Biostatistics

Statistics for Biological & Medical Data

Biostatistics applies statistical reasoning to biological, medical, and health-related questions. This section explores probability, statistical inference, experimental design, modelling, and interpretation of biological data.

Writing inspiration: The challenge of making reliable conclusions from noisy, variable, and often limited biological data.

Topics may include probability distributions, hypothesis testing, estimation, regression, survival analysis, experimental design, and statistical interpretation.

Writing Philosophy

The purpose of these articles is not simply to collect information, but to understand it well enough to explain it clearly.

Each piece aims to connect theory → intuition → mathematics → computation → application.

Where appropriate, articles will include datasets, visualisations, code, references, experiments, external lectures, and links to longer-form publications.
