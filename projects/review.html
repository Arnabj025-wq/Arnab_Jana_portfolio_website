Review Projects

A collection of literature reviews, technical reviews, methodological comparisons, and structured analyses of research and developments across data science, artificial intelligence, statistics, finance, and computational methods.

Review Framework

My review projects follow a structured approach designed to move from understanding individual sources toward a broader synthesis of the research area.

  1. Define the review question
  2. Identify relevant literature
  3. Study and classify sources
  4. Extract important concepts
  5. Compare methodologies
  6. Identify common findings
  7. Identify limitations and gaps
  8. Synthesize the evidence
  9. Develop conclusions and future directions
