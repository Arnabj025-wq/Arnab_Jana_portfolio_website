More

John Tukey

John Tukey was an American mathematician whose work had a major influence on modern statistics and exploratory data analysis.

His philosophy of questioning data carefully before applying sophisticated methods is particularly relevant to data science and statistical research.

The idea behind this quote is simple: a precise answer is not particularly useful if the question itself is poorly formulated.
